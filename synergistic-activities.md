# Appendix 5: Synergistic Activities

**Name:** James Doss-Gollin
**Title and Department:** Assistant Professor, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering
**Institution:** Rice University

- **Use-inspired research with industry and operational partners.** I develop methods that respond directly to decisions faced by infrastructure owners, utilities, and emergency managers. Recent examples include the Consortium for Enhancing Resilience and Catastrophe Modeling (CERCat), where I work with reinsurers and modeling firms on tropical-cyclone hazard assessment (CATRISK26 panel, Feb 2026); external technical review for the Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI); and the Texas Water Development Board's Flood Research Review Panel, which sets the state's flood-research agenda.

- **Open, reproducible, and reusable scientific software.** I release all code and data from my publications as open-source software on GitHub so that results can be independently verified and reused. I contribute to the methodological literature on this practice, including *Unlocking the Benefits of Transparent and Reusable Science for Climate Risk Management* (Pollack et al., *Earth's Future*, 2026), which documents tooling and workflow patterns that make computational research auditable and reusable across modeling groups and national laboratories.

- **Technical communication for emergency response and decision-makers.** I translate complex flood and hydroclimate science for non-academic audiences during and after extreme events. Following the July 2025 Central Texas floods I provided technical commentary to the *Wall Street Journal*, Associated Press, ABC13, KVUE, and Fox26 Houston explaining flood-warning system performance and hydrologic causes; subsequent print interviews include the *Wall Street Journal* on disaster-risk scoring in real-estate markets (Jan 2026) and Telemundo Houston on FEMA flood-risk mapping (Feb 2026).

- **Service to the federal and professional research enterprise.** I serve on the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE-EWRI) Environmental and Water Resources Systems Committee, the American Geophysical Union *Water and Society* Newsletter Committee, and I am a working-group member of the World Climate Research Programme (WCRP) *Digital Earths* Lighthouse Activity working group on *Digital Twins -- Beyond Physical Systems* (2026--2030). I review proposals for the National Science Foundation and the Department of Energy Biological and Environmental Research program, and I have served as a peer reviewer for more than 20 journals (2023 Outstanding Reviewer, *Earth's Future*).

- **Convening interdisciplinary research communities.** I organize technical forums that connect academic researchers with practitioners in industry and government. At AGU 2025 I co-convened five sessions on *Advances in Urban Flood Risk Assessment and Adaptation* and two sessions on *Interdisciplinary Advances in Catastrophe Modeling and Disaster Resilience: Bridging Science, Policy, and Practice*. I co-organized the *Nature-Based Solutions for a Resilient Gulf Coast* workshop at Rice (March 2025) and previously convened AGU sessions on hybrid modeling and digital-twin systems for flood-hazard prediction, household water security, and operational climate services.
